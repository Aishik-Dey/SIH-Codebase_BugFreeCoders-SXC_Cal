OVERALL MASTER CONCLUSIONS
==========================

1. Belief/UCB learning is the most robustly validated improvement:
   large gains over open-loop scanning, with 10/10 multi-seed wins.

2. Multi-channel scanning strongly increases interception.

3. PRI awareness has a specialized EW advantage: sparse periodic emitters
   can be captured when Markov/UCB misses them.

4. Original PRI lock-on was seed-sensitive because the 8-hit requirement
   was too close to expected warmup observations.

5. Persistent V4 is the strongest robustness fix for sparse radar:
   30.0 +/- 48.3% -> 93.3 +/- 0.9% radar capture in the 10-seed validation,
   with >50% capture seeds improving 3/10 -> 10/10.

6. V4 has an explicit resource-allocation cost: communication-emitter
   capture falls substantially.

7. Plateau handling improves k=2 relative to the original PRIAware.

8. The +/-1 off-by-one candidate expansion has negligible effect on beacon
   aggregate interception and does not explain the k=2 regression.

9. Fractional/harmonic PRI handling is validated on synthetic PDW:
   band-4 correct lock improves 70% -> 95%.

10. Switch cost is a major system constraint. Frequent switching can make
    otherwise good schedulers miss pulses during settling.

11. The sparse-radar warmup/dwell fix should NOT be blindly generalized to
    beacon scenarios. Its added warmup causes a measurable regression there.

12. The actual Turing/TSRD dataset has not yet been tested in this run.
